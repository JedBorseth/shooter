# Scope

The game is a tight 6v6 multiplayer shooter: Call of Duty's movement and
gunplay, its core modes, and bots to fill the teams. This page lists what is in
the game and what was cut on purpose. Build only what is listed under **In**.
Anything else needs the owner's yes first, even if Call of Duty has it.

## In

- **Movement:** sprint, slide, jump, mantle, ladders, prone, and weapon mounting ([docs/movement.md](docs/movement.md)).
- **Weapons:** the fourteen guns, attachments, frag, flashbang, smoke, and the knife ([docs/weapons.md](docs/weapons.md)).
- **Modes:** Team Deathmatch, Free-for-all, Gun Game, Capture the Flag, Hardpoint, Domination, and Search and Destroy ([docs/modes.md](docs/modes.md)).
- **Maps:** Breakwater, the one multiplayer map, plus the Testing Area for practice ([docs/maps.md](docs/maps.md)).
- **Create-a-Class:** Pick 10, perks, and wildcards ([docs/create-a-class.md](docs/create-a-class.md)).
- **Scorestreaks:** UAV, Counter-UAV, and Care Package, with the minimap ([docs/scorestreaks.md](docs/scorestreaks.md)).
- **Progression:** XP, 55 levels, and the unlocks they bring ([docs/progression.md](docs/progression.md)).
- **Killcams** ([docs/killcam.md](docs/killcam.md)).
- **Bots** at three skills in every mode ([docs/bots.md](docs/bots.md)).
- **Custom games:** match rules and bots per team ([docs/custom-games.md](docs/custom-games.md)).
- **Playing online:** a dedicated server, joined by typing its address; the launcher and the Docker server image ([docs/multiplayer.md](docs/multiplayer.md), [docs/server.md](docs/server.md)).
- **Settings, HUD, audio, and art** for all of the above.
